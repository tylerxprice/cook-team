# 06: Step 4 Schedule Broadcast, Auto-Triggered Common Meal Sign-Up Workbook Export & On-Demand Data Export

**What to build:** In Step 4 of the Coordinator Wizard, **Meal Coordinators** can send the monthly schedule announcement email (and optional survey invitations) via Gmail SMTP (`nodemailer` + App Password) or click **"Mark as Broadcast Sent"**. Doing either transitions the **Planning Month** status to `EMAIL_SENT`, records broadcast metadata in Postgres, and automatically exports the finalized schedule into a new month tab on the community's **Common Meal Sign-Up Workbook** in Google Sheets (using default meal deadlines and day labels if not already exported for that month, while still allowing the coordinator to open the customization modal to tweak deadlines/labels beforehand or re-export later). Additionally, **Meal Coordinators** can click an on-demand **"Export to Google Sheets"** button to write clean, human-readable `Members`, `Availability_YYYY-MM` (including `willing_to_mentor` and custom question columns), and `Schedule_YYYY-MM` tabs to Google Sheets via the Service Account, and a Playwright E2E test suite verifies the Next.js user journeys end-to-end.

**Blocked by:** 04: Coordinator Scheduling Wizard (Steps 1–3), Matchmaker Solver & Authenticated Schedule View, 05: Idempotent Google Sheets → Supabase Importer & Community Equity Reports

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Step 4 generates the schedule announcement email text (including the link to the authenticated Next.js schedule view for that **Planning Month**) and sends it via the `nodemailer` Gmail SMTP adapter to the configured `announcementTo` and `announcementCc` recipients.
- [ ] Sending the schedule announcement email OR clicking **"Mark as Broadcast Sent"** updates `planning_months.status` to `EMAIL_SENT`, records `broadcast_sent_at`, `broadcast_sent_by`, `broadcast_to`, and `broadcast_subject`, and automatically triggers the **Common Meal Sign-Up Workbook** export via the Google Sheets Service Account adapter (if not yet exported for that month).
- [ ] **Meal Coordinators** can also open the **Common Meal Sign-Up Workbook** configuration modal in Step 4 to customize per-meal deadlines or day labels, preview the columns, and manually trigger or re-trigger the workbook export at any time.
- [ ] **Meal Coordinators** can trigger an on-demand data export to Google Sheets that writes clean `Members`, `Availability_YYYY-MM` (with `willing_to_mentor` and all `custom_questions` / `custom_answers` as columns), and `Schedule_YYYY-MM` tabs without relying on legacy GAS internal settings formats.
- [ ] Integration tests against local Supabase Postgres with injected SMTP and Google Sheets adapters verify that both sending the announcement email and marking it as broadcast automatically invoke the **Common Meal Sign-Up Workbook** export and record dispatch state, and verify the on-demand `Members` / `Availability_YYYY-MM` / `Schedule_YYYY-MM` export payload structure.
- [ ] Playwright E2E tests verify the Next.js application flows (unrecognized user **"Request Access"**, signed-in **Member** profile + **Availability Survey** + schedule view, and **Meal Coordinator** 4-step wizard) alongside the passing GAS E2E suite.
